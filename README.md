# Wildline: Northreach Reserve

A browser-based, first-person hunting game set in Northreach Reserve. Travel from your station by truck, track wildlife, hunt, recover animals, load your truck and return to sell your harvest. Earn money for equipment and unlock ten expeditions across daytime and nighttime environments, from guided training to the final tower hunt.

Use the Equipment Shop to buy mission equipment, including the Trailmaster semi-auto, med kits, leg-hold traps and a fishing rod. Follow wildlife markers and the forest map, and resume or replay expeditions through **Jobs**. All ten jobs unlock in sequence.

**My stats** is available from the main menu from the start. Completed jobs reveal their recorded results, including attempts, completions, best times, animals hunted, shots fired and earnings. Unfinished jobs show no results. Additional mission details appear when recorded in your save.

## Jobs

1. **First Hunt** — A guided expedition teaching driving, aiming, shooting, recovering, loading and selling your first animal.
2. **Hunt 4 rabbits** — Hunt and recover four rabbits in Foxglove Meadow before time runs out.
3. **Hunt 3 wild boars** — Face three tough boars in Alder Hollow. Their weaving charges demand careful aim and movement.
4. **Hunt 2 deer at night** — Buy and equip the Trailmaster semi-auto, then hunt two deer on Stag Ridge. Killing the first deer triggers an ambush by five wolves; defeat the pack and recover both deer.
5. **Hunt 8 geese** — Hunt the flock at Willow Pond. Disturbing one alerts them all, sending the geese into circling flight.
6. **Hunt 6 alligators** — Hunt at Cypress Pond, where three alligators attack together. Each takes two shots within 60 metres.
7. **Hunt 2 rhinos** — Purchase a med kit before entering Ironwood Clearing. Both rhinos charge together, dealing 40 health damage per hit. Each requires five shots within 48 metres.
8. **Bait and hunt the lioness** — Buy five leg-hold traps and a med kit. Set traps in Foxglove Meadow at night, harvest the rabbits at dawn and place them as bait in Redfern Clearing. Return to the station laptop and wait 10 seconds, then hunt the fast-charging lioness. She takes four shots within 100 metres and deals 35 health damage per charge. Recover and sell her to finish. After an attempt, replay the full expedition or start directly at the lioness hunt.
9. **Wolf tower watch and bear hunt** — Climb the watchtower at night and protect the pond from five packs of four wolves. Each wolf takes one shot; feeding wolves slowly deplete the fish supply. Losing all fish fails the job. Packs arrive 10 seconds after the previous pack is cleared. At dawn, buy a fishing rod, then hold E at the pond bank for five seconds to catch and load twenty fish. Place them one by one as bait in Briarwood Basin, return to the station laptop and wait 10 seconds. The bear charges as fast as the lioness, takes five shots within 60 metres and deals 50 health damage per charge. Recover and sell it to finish. After an attempt, replay the full expedition or start directly at the bear hunt.
10. **The Grand Hunt** — Flooding beyond the reserve sends migrating herds past the watchtower. Hunt in daylight with no time limit or collection: 20 flying geese, 25 boars, 30 rabbits, 25 wolves, 15 deer, 8 alligators, 6 rhinos, 6 lionesses and 5 bears. A left-side tracker shows each quota. Animals arrive in random groups, missed targets return, and completed species stop spawning. Geese fly left to right in front of the tower near the pond, below the roof-obstructed sightline. Each animal takes one shot; fallen animals remain on the ground. Fulfil all 140 targets to conquer Northreach Reserve, then replay jobs or review your stats from the main menu.
