## Recorded audio — no generated oscillators or noise

- Rifle / carbine: [Gunshot sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds), Tabasco, CC0. Individual shots trimmed from the Mosin and SKS recordings; playback levels adjusted.
- Reload and bolt: [Gun reload sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds), SpringySpringo, CC0.
- Engine: [Racing car engine sound loops](https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops), domasx2, CC0. Playback speed follows vehicle speed.
- Collision: [Crash Collision](https://opengameart.org/content/crash-collision), Iwan “qubodup” Gabovitch, CC0. Recorded racing-game impact, low-pass filtered and mixed at a restrained volume.
- Footsteps: [Fantozzi’s footsteps](https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone), Fantozzi / qubodup, CC0. Six sand footstep recordings. Recorded footsteps also provide landing and body-impact feedback.
- Birds / wind: [Birds and Wind Ambient](https://opengameart.org/content/birds-and-wind-ambient-birds-wind-and-synth), Spring Spring; source recordings credited to isaiah658, syncopika and pauliuw, CC0. Uses the birds-and-wind version, not the synth version; mixed quietly.
- Music: [Sunset Plains](https://opengameart.org/content/sunset-plains), Yoiyami, CC0. Quiet guitar-and-pad background track.
