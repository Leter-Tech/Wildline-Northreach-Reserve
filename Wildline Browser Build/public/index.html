Forest notes / 01

Move quietly.
Watch carefully.

Park outside the hunting ground. Follow the amber bearing on your compass. A nearby running vehicle can alert one animal within 48 metres. Park at a distance and approach quietly.

Crouch into cover. Hold Shift to open your optic and move the mouse to aim. Release Shift to lower it. Aim behind the shoulder for a vital hit.

An empty magazine can be reloaded with R. The hunt only fails when magazine and reserve are both exhausted before your quota, an animal escapes, or the time limit closes. Gold marks one living target; the minimap shows all wildlife.

Approach your harvest and hold E to collect it. Load it at the rear of the truck, drive home, and sell at the station’s forest desk.
